# Managing Triggers

## Managing Triggers

## Rename a Trigger

To rename a trigger in the automation, perform the steps given below:

1.  Click the trigger tab (in our case, **HTTP Request Trigger**).![Select_HTTP_Trigger.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltf3b029f57a47da66/699bc65b676f8800085c09df/Select_HTTP_Trigger.png)
2.  Click the edit icon visible on the trigger header to rename trigger.  
    ![Edit_Trigger_Icon.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltf59cc50dcfc19b61/699bc65b31a5c5000890e910/Edit_Trigger_Icon.png)
3.  The **HTTP Request Trigger** field becomes editable. Update the name as required and click the “✔” check mark.  
    ![Edit_Trigger_Name.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltdc8ad141abda46f0/699bc65b8d3a6a0008c5b1f5/Edit_Trigger_Name.png)

## Delete a Trigger

To delete a trigger in the automation, perform the steps given below:

1.  Click the trigger tab (in our case, **HTTP Request Trigger**).  
    ![Select_HTTP_Trigger.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltf3b029f57a47da66/699bc65b676f8800085c09df/Select_HTTP_Trigger.png)
2.  Click the delete icon visible on the trigger header to delete the trigger.  
    ![Delete_Icon.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt5a2fdc0d56d0fc83/699bc65b624a07000845fd5e/Delete_Icon.png)
3.  Confirm your action by clicking **Delete** again.  
    ![Delete_Trigger_Modal.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltaccf2d44704f1c9a/699bc65c31a5c5000890e914/Delete_Trigger_Modal.png)
4.  You can also delete the trigger from the configured trigger as shown below:  
    ![Delete_trigger_from_configuration_step.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt32a26f77045c475b/699bc65b0c8fb20008e60801/Delete_trigger_from_configuration_step.png)

This will remove the trigger from the **automations** configuration page.
