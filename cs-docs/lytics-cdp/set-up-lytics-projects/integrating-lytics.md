# Integrating Lytics

## Integrating Lytics

Connect Lytics to your CMS, Launch, and Personalize products so behavioral data and audiences flow automatically across your stack.

## How It Connects to Your Stack

| **Product** | **Role** |
| --- | --- |
| Contentstack CMS | Classifies content and syncs entries with Lytics topics and audience affinities |
| Contentstack Launch | Deploys JSTag to your front-end environment for behavioral data collection |
| Contentstack Personalize | Receives Lytics audiences for variant targeting and A/B tests |

Together, these form a continuous feedback loop: behavioral data flows from your front end through JSTag into Lytics, where audiences are built, then pushed into Personalize to drive dynamic content delivery.

## What You Need Before Getting Started

Before configuring the integration, ensure you have:

-   Access to your Contentstack organization as an Owner or Admin. Learn more about [administration roles](/docs/administration/about-administration-roles).
-   Lytics enabled for your organization - [contact Contentstack Support](https://www.contentstack.com/support)
-   A website or app where JSTag can be deployed
