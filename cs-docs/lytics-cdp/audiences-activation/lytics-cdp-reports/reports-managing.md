# Managing Reports

## Managing Reports

Reports come fully equipped to manage your Reports across your team.

#### Editing and Deleting Reports

Users can quickly _Edit_ and _Delete_ reports at the top of the Report page. The _Edit_ option allows you to rename the Report _name_ and _description_.

![shortcode-img-0032.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/am45b6dd7d8e51bed8/b4730fda541242d80ae47a16/shortcode-img-0032.png)

#### Sharing Your Reports

Creating Audience Reports in Lytics can be a collaborative process. By default, Reports within Lytics are shared across all users with the.`Reporting` role. To prohibit users from accessing Reports globally the.`Reporting` role can be disabled for specific users.

In addition to the global role, a report creator can further restrict access to their reports by setting the report as`private` and adding the specific list of users who have access. The screenshot below outlines the simple setup process to fine-tune collaboration and access to individual reports.

![img-0260.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/amde7ab98ed71b2538/248d977479b6676025700d2d/img-0260.png)

#### Change Logs

Lytics records a log of all changes made to a Report in the “Logs” tab. This record lets users keep track of who updated a report's components.

#### Downloading Reports

Each component in Lytics provides the option to download the associated data in a CSV format. The _Download_ icon is located in the bottom right of each component:
