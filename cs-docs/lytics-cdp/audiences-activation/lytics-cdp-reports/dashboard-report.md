# Dashboard Report

## Dashboard Report

How to interpret and configure your Dashboard Report

Once data is flowing into your account, Lytics will automatically generate a Dashboard [Report](/docs/lytics/getting-started-with-reports) on the homepage of the Decision Engine app. While the dashboard Report is fully customizable, the out-of-the-box Report consists of 4 [Report Components](/docs/lytics/reports-components) that incorporate some of Lytics' [Behavioral Audiences :](/docs/lytics/descriptive-predictive-modeling#lytics-behavioral-audiences)

1.  **Size Component** of the _All_, _Lytics New_, and _Lytics Highly Engaged_ audiences
2.  **Dataflow Component** of the 5 most "used" audiences (ie the audiences that appear in the most Jobs).
3.  **Composition Component** of Lytics' Content Affinity
4.  **Overlap Component** of the _All_, _Lytics New_ and _Lytics Highly Engaged_ audiences.

To modify or edit the Dashboard Report, simply click on the **Edit Dashboard** button located on the top-right corner of the page. Clicking this button will redirect you to the Report page, where you can add or delete Components, and edit existing Components.
