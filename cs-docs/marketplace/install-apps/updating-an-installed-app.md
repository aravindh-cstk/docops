# Updating an Installed App

## Updating an Installed App

To update an app, follow the steps below:

1.  Navigate to Contentstack Marketplace.
2.  Click **Manage** in the left-side navigation panel, and select **Installed Apps**.
3.  All apps with an available update are highlighted with a red dot.  
    ![Apps-With-Updates](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blta5928b55589b003d/64de7939992f3c05a7869c1d/Apps-With-Updates.png)
4.  Click the app icon, then you can see a modal with details such as **Overview** (describes the app, its features and other information), **Screenshots** (shows few screenshots of the app), **Use Cases** (provides scenarios where an app can be used), and **Installed On** (includes the stack name, installed app version, who has installed the app, and the time of installation). By default, you are on the **Installed On** tab.  
    ![Installed-On-Modal](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltcdf17f8735b00a56/64de7939992f3cc3e4869c21/Installed-On-Modal.png)  
    The red dot after the stack name signifies that an app update is available.
5.  Click **Update** to install the app updates.  
    ![Update-Icon-And-Button.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt435eaa0bd0e18e52/64de79391a23ce0b3f5b9cac/Update-Icon-And-Button.png)
6.  In the popup window, the stack is, by default, selected, accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and click the **Update** button.  
    ![Install-Updates](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltca2e11038045db1a/64de79394eb7009d2df7eb38/Install-Updates.png)
7.  Optionally, you can also update the existing configuration details which were configured when you first installed the app.  
    ![Configuration](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt201d7433ff09c173/64de7938adbf21436287d3df/Configuration.png)
8.  Click the **Save** button to save the updated configuration details.
9.  Click the **Open Stack** button to directly navigate to the stack.
10.  Click the **Cancel** button to cancel updating the configuration details.![Cancel-And-Open-Stack-And-Save-Buttons](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt959de12e572501b3/64de79395acb3b16d8301a94/Cancel-And-Open-Stack-And-Save-Buttons.png)
