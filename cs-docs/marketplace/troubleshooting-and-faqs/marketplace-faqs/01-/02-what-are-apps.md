# What are Apps?

Apps are a new type of integration provided by Contentstack that allows you to connect quickly with third party services. [Apps](/marketplace) are enhanced versions of extensions and can be [installed](/docs/marketplace/installing-apps) and [uninstalled](/docs/marketplace/uninstalling-an-app) easily. We support two types of apps, organization apps and stack apps.
