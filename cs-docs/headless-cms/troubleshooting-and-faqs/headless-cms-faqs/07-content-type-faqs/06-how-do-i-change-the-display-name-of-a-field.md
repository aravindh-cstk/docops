# How do I change the display name of a field?

When you [create a content type](/docs/headless-cms/create-a-content-type) and add a field, follow these steps to change its display name:

1.  Click the “Properties” (gear) icon for the field.
2.  Enter a suitable title under the [Display Name](/docs/headless-cms/display-name) property.
