# Can I have a Preview Token without a Delivery Token?

No. [Preview Tokens](/docs/headless-cms/about-delivery-tokens#understanding-preview-tokens) are linked with Delivery Tokens. You must first [create a Delivery Token](/docs/headless-cms/create-a-delivery-token) and [generate a Preview Token](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/create-a-delivery-token#create-a-preview-token-for-an-existing-delivery-token).
