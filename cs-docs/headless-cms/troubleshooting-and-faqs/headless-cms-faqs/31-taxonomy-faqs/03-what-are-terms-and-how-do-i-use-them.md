# What are terms, and how do I use them?

Terms are the building blocks for categorizing entries. You can define them in a hierarchical structure and associate them with entries.

Learn more about [creating terms](/docs/headless-cms/create-a-term) and [adding terms to entries](/docs/headless-cms/adding-terms-to-entries-with-taxonomy).
