# Can I manage labels in Contentstack?

Yes, you can create, edit, rename, nest, and delete labels in Contentstack. For detailed instructions, refer to the [Manage Labels](https://contentstack-com.contentstackapps.com/docs/headless-cms/manage-labels/) documentation.
