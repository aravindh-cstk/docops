# Who has permission to create, edit, or delete environments in Contentstack?

Only the stack [Admin](/docs/headless-cms/types-of-roles#admin) and [Developer](/docs/headless-cms/types-of-roles#developer) can [create](/docs/headless-cms/add-an-environment), [edit](/docs/headless-cms/edit-an-environment), or [delete](/docs/headless-cms/delete-an-environment) an environment.
