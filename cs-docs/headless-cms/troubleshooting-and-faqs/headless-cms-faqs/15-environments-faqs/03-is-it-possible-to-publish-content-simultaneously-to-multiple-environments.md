# Is it possible to publish content simultaneously to multiple environments?

Yes, you can [publish](/docs/headless-cms/publish-an-entry) content simultaneously to multiple environments, such as **development**, **staging**, and **production**. This simplifies your workflow and ensures content consistency across environments.
