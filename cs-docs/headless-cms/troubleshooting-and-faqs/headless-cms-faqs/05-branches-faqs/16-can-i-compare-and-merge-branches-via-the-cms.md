# Can I compare and merge branches via the CMS?

No, currently the compare and merge feature is only available via [CMA requests](/docs/developers/apis/content-management-api/#comparing-branches) or [CLI commands.](/docs/headless-cms/compare-and-merge-branches-using-the-cli/)
