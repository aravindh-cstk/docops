# Can I add comments to a Global field or Group field as a whole?

No, comments can only be added to the subfields of "Group" and "Global Fields", but not to the parent field as a whole. For more information, refer to the [Add a Comment](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/add-a-comment) document.
