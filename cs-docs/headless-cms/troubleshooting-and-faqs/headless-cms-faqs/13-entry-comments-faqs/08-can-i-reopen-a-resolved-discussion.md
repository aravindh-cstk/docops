# Can I reopen a resolved discussion?

Yes, you can reopen a resolved discussion if there is no active discussion on the field it was originally linked to. For more information, refer to the [Reopen a Discussion](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/reopen-a-discussion) document.
