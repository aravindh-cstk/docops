# Can we bulk publish multiple entries at once?

Yes. Currently, you can [bulk publish multiple entries](/docs/headless-cms/bulk-publish-entries). Check out the [Publish an Entry](/docs/headless-cms/publish-an-entry) section to know more.

You can also bulk publish multiple entries with nested references at once. Check out our [Bulk Publish Entries with Nested References](/docs/headless-cms/bulk-publish-entries/#bulk-publish-entries-with-nested-references) documentation to know more.
