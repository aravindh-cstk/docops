# Can I assign different permissions to different asset folders?

Yes, you can assign distinct [permissions](/docs/headless-cms/create-a-role#permissions-on-assets/) to different asset folders. For example, you could grant “Read” permission to one folder (e.g., Folder 1) and “Publish” permission to another (e.g., Folder 2), depending on your organizational needs.
