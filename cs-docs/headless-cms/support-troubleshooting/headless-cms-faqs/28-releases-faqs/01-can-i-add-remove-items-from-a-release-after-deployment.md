# Can I add/remove items from a Release after deployment?

Yes, you can add or remove items from a release even after deployment using the unlock feature. Once you [unlock](/docs/headless-cms/unlock-a-release) the release, you can modify its contents as needed. After making your changes, you can [lock](/docs/headless-cms/lock-a-release) the release again.
