# What happens to old discussions after they are resolved?

Resolved discussions remain accessible in the **Resolved** section of the **Discussions** tab for future reference. For more information, refer to the [View Discussions Timeline](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/view-discussions-timeline) document.
