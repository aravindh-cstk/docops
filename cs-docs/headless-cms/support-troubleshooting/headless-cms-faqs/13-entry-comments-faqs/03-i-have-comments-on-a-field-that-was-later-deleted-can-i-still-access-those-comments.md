# I have comments on a field that was later deleted. Can I still access those comments?

If you delete a field with associated comments, both the field and its comments will no longer appear in the entry editor. The discussion automatically resolves and moves to the **Resolved** section in the **Discussions** tab. You can access these details anytime through the Discussions tab in the right navigation panel. For more information, refer to the [Entry Comments](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/about-entry-comments/) document.
