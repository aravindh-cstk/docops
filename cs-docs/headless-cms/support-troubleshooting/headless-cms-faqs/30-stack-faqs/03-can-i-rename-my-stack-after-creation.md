# Can I rename my Stack after creation?

Yes, the stack [Owner](/docs/headless-cms/types-of-roles#owner) or [Admin](/docs/headless-cms/types-of-roles#admin) can [edit a stack’s details](/docs/headless-cms/edit-a-stack).
