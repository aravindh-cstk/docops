# Why should I use Management Tokens instead of Authtokens?

Use Management Tokens for automation, CI/CD pipelines, external integrations, or in [Single Sign-On](/docs/administration/about-single-sign-on-sso) environments. This avoids the risk of exposing personal [authtokens](/docs/headless-cms/types-of-tokens#authentication-tokens-auth-tokens).
