# How do I get started with Contentstack?

You will first need a Contentstack account to get started. Once you have an account, read our docs to understand how Contentstack works. Developers who are well aware of Contentstack and are interested in creating websites and apps can get started with our docs on various [platforms](/docs/developers#development-resources). While new users, content managers, and editors can go through the basic concepts of creating a website or app in Contentstack [](/docs/headless-cms)in our [Content Manager's Guide](/docs/headless-cms).
