# Why cannot I see any stages to select a specific stage in the “Next available stages” section?

First, you need to add all the [stages](/docs/headless-cms/about-workflow-stages) at once, and later on you can browse through **Stage transition rules** to select a specific stage under the **Next available stages** section.
