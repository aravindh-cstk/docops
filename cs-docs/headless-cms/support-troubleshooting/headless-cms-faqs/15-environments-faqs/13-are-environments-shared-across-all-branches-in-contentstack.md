# Are environments shared across all branches in Contentstack?

Yes, environments are [global modules](/docs/headless-cms/global-modules) available across all [branches](/docs/headless-cms/about-branches) of your [stack](/docs/headless-cms/about-stack). Any environment created in the main branch will be accessible in all other branches.
