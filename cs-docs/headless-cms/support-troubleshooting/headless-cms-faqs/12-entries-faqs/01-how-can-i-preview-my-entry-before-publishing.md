# How can I preview my entry before publishing?

Contentstack offers [Live Preview](/docs/headless-cms/about-live-preview/) functionality, enabling you to review your entry content across various channels before saving or publishing it on your live website. This feature allows you to edit an entry and observe content changes simultaneously in real-time.
