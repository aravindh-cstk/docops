# Save Your Views

## Save Your Views

The Views feature in Contentstack allows you to save your search settings for easy access later.

To save a view, log in to your [Contentstack account](https://www.contentstack.com/login) and follow these steps:

1.  Navigate to your [stack](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/about-stack) and select the “[Entries](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/about-entries)” or “[Assets](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/about-assets)” module.
2.  By default, you will see the **Views** tab in the left panel. Here, you will see a list of **Popular Views**. Consider you select the **Last Modified by Me** view.![Save Your View_1.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt2bee2a300ce52ff7/67d96dd85baf486ea03af4b9/1._Save_Your_Views-_image_1.png)
3.  Apply filters or perform [advanced search](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/headless-cms/advanced-search) to further refine the **Last Modified by Me** search results. Here are the list of changes or preference that you can apply to the entries or assets list page and save the view:
    -   Search text results
    -   Applied filters
    -   Advanced search results
    -   Customized filters preferences (applied through “Manage Filters”)
    -   Changes made to table columns (custom column widths, show/hide/freeze columns, reorder columns, etc.)
4.  In the top-right corner of the results page, click the drop-down menu showing the current view name (e.g., "Last Modified by Me") and select **Save as new view**.![2._Save_Your_Views-image_2.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltba58a6307dd4b060/67d96dd82a70955f1d8ca6a3/2._Save_Your_Views-image_2.png)
5.  In the **Save View** modal, enter the **Name** of the view and click **Save**.![Save Your View_3.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blta3c96f55e6710259/67d96dd8d0196dfec770b969/3._Save_Your_Views-image_3.png)
6.  Your saved view is now listed under **Saved Views** in the left panel.![Save Your View_4.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt840674736f1ab644/67d96dd817c8008fc1cda844/4._Save_Your_Views-image_4.png)

**Note:** The views you save are only visible to you, ensuring your work remains private. However, if you share a view, collaborators can access it and save it as a view.
