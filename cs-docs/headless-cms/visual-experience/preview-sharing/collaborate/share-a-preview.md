# Share a Preview

## Share a Preview

The **Share Preview** feature in Contentstack’s **Visual Editor** enables teams to preview content across devices and collaborate effectively before publishing. Stakeholders can visualize updates in real time, provide feedback, and ensure alignment with design and content goals—resulting in a smoother, error-free publishing process.

To share a preview, log in to your [Contentstack account](https://www.contentstack.com/login/), and perform the following steps:

1.  Go to your [stack](/docs/headless-cms/about-stack) where you want to preview and manage your website.
2.  In the left panel, click the **Visual Experience** icon. By default you would see the Timeline preview. Click **Editor** from the pill at the bottom to switch to **Visual Editor**.
3.  Use the website navigation menu or the **URL** bar to find the page you want to share.
4.  From the options available under the horizontal ellipsis, click the **Share** button.![1. Share Button.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt632a92e466844f8b/67f95d149e940a268966a00d/1._Share_Button.png)

    **Note:** The Share button requires **Live Preview SDK v3.2.0** or later. If you’re using an older version of the SDK, the sharing functionality will not be available.

5.  In the **Share Preview** modal that appears, add the following details:
    1.  **Invite User(s):** Add the email addresses of the users you want to invite.
    2.  **Add a Note:** Provide optional instructions or details for the recipients.
6.  Click **Share** to send invitations.![2. Share Preview Modal.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltfb8305bac6445ee4/67f95d149e940a023466a009/2._Share_Preview_Modal.png)
7.  The recipients will receive an email with the **preview link**.![3. Invitation Email.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltc747258fd9fcdade/67f95d15787e78ce993c343e/3._Invitation_Email.png)

    **Note:** The preview link is valid for **7 days**.


The Share Preview feature simplifies content collaboration by providing secure, easy-to-share previews. With password protection and cross-platform support, teams can streamline feedback collection, enhance communication, and ensure a polished final product before publishing.
