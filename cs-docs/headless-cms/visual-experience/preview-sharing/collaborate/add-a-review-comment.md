# Add a Review Comment

## Add a Review Comment

Reviewers can provide feedback by adding comments to specific sections of the shared content. This feature promotes collaboration and ensures that all stakeholders contribute to content refinement.

**Note:** Ensure that a preview of the content has been shared. Refer to the [Share a Preview](/docs/headless-cms/share-a-preview) document for more information.

To add a review comment, perform the following steps:

1.  Open the **shared preview link** from your email.

    **Note:** When sharing a preview with external users who are not added to the stack or do not have a Contentstack account, they will be prompted to enter a display name before they can view or comment on the content.

    ![1. Set Display Name.png](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/blt570b7a17ad0066a7/67f95c74e3fc57b6771a50c5/1._Set_Display_Name.png)
2.  Click the section where you want to add feedback.
3.  In the **Add New Comment** modal, enter your comment.
4.  Click **Post** to submit your comment.

    **Note:** The maximum length of a comment is **500 characters**.

5.  To edit a comment you’ve posted, click the **Edit** icon, make your changes, and then click **Update** to save them.
6.  To delete a comment, click the **Delete** icon.

    **Note:** If your comment is the only one in a discussion thread and you delete it, the entire thread will be removed. Deleted comments cannot be recovered.

    ![2. Review Comments GIF.gif](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt2d43f51baca745a8/bltf198128c065c489f/67f960ed4f7fafdbe948a971/2._Review_Comments_GIF.gif)

After you add a comment, a discussion thread is created in the **Discussions** panel. Team members can reply, continue the conversation, or mark comments as resolved as needed.
