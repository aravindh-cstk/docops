# Change Your Subscription or Add-Ons

## Change Your Subscription or Add-Ons

When you need more of something, you have two ways to get it. Move to a higher subscription option to raise everything at once, or buy an add-on to raise one limit while staying on your current subscription.

Which one you need depends on the resource. Metered resources such as API requests, bandwidth, and AI credits are governed by your subscription and by extra usage. Fixed resources such as users, stacks, and entries are raised with add-ons.

## Which Approach to Choose

-   **Change your subscription** when you need more of several things at once, or when you need a feature your current subscription does not include, such as custom roles or custom workflows.
-   **Buy an add-on** when you need more of one thing and the rest of your subscription suits you. Adding two users costs less than moving to a higher subscription option for the sake of two users.
-   **Allow extra usage** when the resource is metered and your need is occasional rather than permanent. Refer to the [Track and Extend Your Subscription Usage](/docs/administration/track-and-extend-your-subscription-usage) documentation.

To compare subscription options and see current pricing, go to the [pricing page](https://www.contentstack.com/pricing).

## Prerequisites

-   [Contentstack account](https://www.contentstack.com/login/)
-   Organization Owner permissions
-   A payment card, for a first upgrade from Free

## Move to a Higher Subscription Option

Moving from Free to a paid subscription option is your first payment, so Contentstack collects your card details on a secure payment page hosted by Stripe, its payment provider. Contentstack does not store your card number.

Once a card is on file, later subscription changes are charged to it directly and you see the amount before you confirm.

To change your subscription, log in to your [Contentstack account](https://www.contentstack.com/login) and perform the steps below:

1.  Click your avatar in the top-right corner, and then click **Manage Subscription**. Contentstack opens the **Subscription** page in your account settings.
2.  Click **Upgrade**. Contentstack opens the **Plans and Pricing** page.
3.  Click the button for the subscription you want, such as **Get Build** or **Get Growth**. Your current subscription is marked **Current plan** and cannot be selected.

4.  Do one of the following:

-   **First payment**: on the payment page, enter your card and billing details, and then confirm the payment.
-   **Card already on file**: review **Review Your Order**, select the checkbox to authorize the charge, and click **Confirm Payment**. To change your selection, click **Go Back**.

Contentstack applies the new subscription immediately and returns you to your subscription. Your invoice arrives by email.

### What Review Your Order Shows

-   How many days remain in the current period, and the proportion you are charged for.
-   A row for the subscription option you are moving to, and a row crediting the unused part of your current subscription.
-   **Charged Today**, the difference between the two.
-   **Recurring Monthly Bill**, what you pay from the next billing date onward. Expand it to see the breakdown.

**Note:** The amount on **Review Your Order** is an estimate calculated at the moment the page loads, from the days remaining in your billing period. That figure keeps changing as the period elapses, so the amount charged can differ slightly if you wait before confirming. To be charged the amount you see, confirm the payment promptly. For the exact figure, check the invoice.

## Buy or Change Add-Ons

Add-ons raise an individual limit without changing your subscription. You can add users, stacks, content types, entries, assets, locales, and custom roles.

**Note:** Add-ons are available on Growth. On other subscription options, the **Add-ons** section does not appear, and you raise limits by changing your subscription instead.

1.  On the **Subscription** page, in the **Add-ons** section, click **Manage Add-Ons**.
2.  For each add-on you want, click the plus icon to increase the quantity or the minus icon to decrease it.

Each card shows what one unit of that add-on gives you, how many units you currently use this month, and the change you are about to make with its monthly cost.

3.  Click **Proceed to Payment**.
4.  Review the amount, and then confirm the payment.

Contentstack applies the new limits and returns you to your subscription.

**Note:** Some add-ons are sold in blocks rather than single units. The quantity selector states the ratio next to each add-on, so check it before you set a quantity.

**Warning:** Reducing an add-on quantity lowers your limit. If you already use more than the reduced limit allows, remove the extra users, stacks, or entries first. Otherwise the reduction fails.

## How Changes Are Billed

Subscription changes and add-ons both take effect immediately and are prorated. Contentstack charges for the days remaining in the current period, then bills the full amount from the next billing date. Add-ons stay on your subscription until you remove them.

## What Happens to Your Content

Changing your subscription or your add-ons does not affect your existing content, users, or API keys. Your stacks, entries, assets, and tokens are unchanged. Only your limits and available features change.

## Moving to a Lower Subscription Option

Moving from one paid subscription option to a lower one is not available in the product. To reduce cost, remove add-ons you no longer need, or contact the [support](mailto:support@contentstack.com) team.
