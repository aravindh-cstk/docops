# How do I get the details of an organization?

To get the details of an organization, log in to your [Contentstack account](https://www.contentstack.com/login) and perform the following steps:

1.  Click on the  "ORGANIZATION" drop-down on the header and select the Organization you want to access.
2.  Click on the “Org Admin” icon on the left navigation panel.
