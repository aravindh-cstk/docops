# I have enabled two-factor authentication (2FA) for my account, but my number has changed. Looks like I am locked out of my account. What should I do?

If you do not have access to the phone number that was used for 2FA registration, you will need to contact our [Support](mailto:support@contentstack.com) team for further assistance.
