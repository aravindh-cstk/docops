# How do I enable SSO for an organization?

To enable [SSO](/docs/administration/about-single-sign-on-sso), you need to meet these two conditions:

-   SSO must be part of your Contentstack plan.
-   You must be the organization [owner](/docs/administration/about-administration-roles), a security manager, or a user with a custom role that has SSO write permissions.

If you meet these two conditions, set up SSO by following the [Set Up SSO in Contentstack](/docs/administration/set-up-sso-in-contentstack) guide. You configure SSO as a connection to your identity provider (IdP). An organization is SSO-enabled once at least one connection is enabled.
