# How do I enable SSO for an organization?

To enable [SSO](/docs/administration/about-single-sign-on-sso), you need to meet these two conditions:

-   You must be the [owner](/docs/administration/about-administration-roles) of the organization
-   SSO must be a part of your Contentstack plan

If you meet these two conditions, you can set up SSO for your organization by following the [Set up SSO](/docs/administration/set-up-sso-in-contentstack) guide.
