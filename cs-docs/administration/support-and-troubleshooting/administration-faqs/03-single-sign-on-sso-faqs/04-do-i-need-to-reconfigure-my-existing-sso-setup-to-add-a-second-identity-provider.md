# Do I need to reconfigure my existing SSO setup to add a second identity provider?

No. Your existing connection keeps its SSO ID, its login URL, and its Assertion Consumer Service (ACS) URL when you add another connection, so the SAML application you already configured at your IdP continues to work without changes.
