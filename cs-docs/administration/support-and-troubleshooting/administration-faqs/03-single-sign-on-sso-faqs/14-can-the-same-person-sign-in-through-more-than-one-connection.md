# Can the same person sign in through more than one connection?

Contentstack identifies a user by email address across every connection in the organization, so the same email address cannot belong to different users in different identity providers. If a person exists in two of your identity providers under the same email address, they reach the same Contentstack user whichever connection they sign in through, and the roles applied come from the connection used for that sign-in.
