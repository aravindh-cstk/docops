# As a user, how do I sign in to an SSO-enabled organization in Contentstack?

To sign in to an SSO-enabled organization, perform the following steps:

1.  Open the [login page](https://www.contentstack.com/login) and click **Log in via SSO**.
2.  Enter the **SSO ID** of the connection you authenticate through, and click **Log in via SSO**. This opens your corporate IdP login page.
3.  Sign in to your Contentstack account by entering your IdP login details.

**Note**: You receive the SSO ID in your stack or organization invitation email. If your organization uses more than one identity provider, your administrator gives you the SSO ID or the login URL for your connection, because the invitation email carries the link for the organization's primary connection only. If you do not know which one to use, contact your organization owner or admin.
