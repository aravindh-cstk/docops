# Can a user with a Custom Role view the settings of a stack?

Users with the [Custom](/docs/headless-cms/types-of-roles#custom-role) or [Content Manager](/docs/headless-cms/types-of-roles#content-manager) role cannot view the full Stack settings, but they may have access to limited or specific settings, depending on their assigned permissions.
