# ​Can a stack have multiple owners?

No, each stack can have only one Owner, who has complete rights to the content and settings of a stack. In addition to that, the Owner has the right to delete a stack as well as [transfer the ownership](/docs/headless-cms/transfer-stack-ownership) of the stack to another user.
