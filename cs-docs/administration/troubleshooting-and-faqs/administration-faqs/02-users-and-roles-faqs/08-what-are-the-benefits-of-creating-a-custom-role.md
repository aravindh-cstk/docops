# ​What are the benefits of creating a Custom role?

The [Custom Role](/docs/headless-cms/types-of-roles#custom-role) gives you the provision to apply permissions at [entry](/docs/headless-cms/about-entries), [field](/docs/headless-cms/about-fields), and [asset](/docs/headless-cms/about-assets) levels.
