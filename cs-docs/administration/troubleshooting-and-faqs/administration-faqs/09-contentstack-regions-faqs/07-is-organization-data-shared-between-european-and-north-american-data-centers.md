# Is organization data shared between European and North American data centers?

**No**. Data is NOT shared between the European and North American data centers. Both the data centers are 100% separate and isolated from each other. Every piece of your [organization](/docs/administration/about-organizations) data resides in your choice of [region](/docs/administration/about-regions/). This means that you cannot decide to store some parts of the organization data in one region and the rest in the other. Once an organization has been registered/ created, you cannot change the organization region.
