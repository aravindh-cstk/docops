# Can I get a longer execution timeout for my application?

Up to a maximum of **13 minutes** (**780 seconds**), provided your application begins streaming a response within **98 seconds**. A fully buffered response (no data until it's complete) still needs to finish within that same 98-second window. Contact your Contentstack representative to discuss your needs.
