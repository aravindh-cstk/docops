# Does Contentstack Launch support forwarding project logs to specific endpoints?

Yes. The [Log Targets](/docs/launch/log-targets) feature in Contentstack Launch allows you to forward the logs produced by [Launch Cloud Functions](/docs/launch/cloud-functions/) or by [Launch-supported frameworks](/docs/launch/launch-framework-support#launch-framework-support-matrix) to third-party monitoring services that support GRPC OTLP endpoint.
