# Does every visit to my site use bandwidth?

No. Requests served from the [CDN cache](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/launch/caching-guide-for-contentstack-launch) do not count toward your Launch bandwidth. Bandwidth is counted when the CDN fetches uncached content from the origin.
