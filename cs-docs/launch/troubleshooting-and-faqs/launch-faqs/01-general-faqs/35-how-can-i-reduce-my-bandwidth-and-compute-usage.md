# How can I reduce my bandwidth and compute usage?

Serving more requests from the CDN cache reduces origin requests and compute usage. [Caching strategy](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/launch/caching-guide-for-contentstack-launch) and application design affect how much compute and bandwidth a project uses. Use [cache revalidation](https://www.contentstack.com/docs/launch/revalidate-cdn-cache) to refresh content without a full redeployment.
