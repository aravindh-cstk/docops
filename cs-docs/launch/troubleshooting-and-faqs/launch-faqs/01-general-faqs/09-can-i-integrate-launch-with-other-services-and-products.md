# Can I integrate Launch with other services and products?

Yes. [Automate](/docs/agent-os/what-is-contentstack-agent-os/) includes triggers and actions for common Launch activities. Launch also includes [deploy hooks](/docs/launch/deploy-hooks/) functionality, allowing you to trigger rebuilds via a URL. Launch can also use the Marketplace app to set up an SSO at the edge.
