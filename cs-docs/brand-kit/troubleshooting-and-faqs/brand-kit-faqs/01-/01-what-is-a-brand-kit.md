# What is a Brand Kit?

[Brand Kit](/docs/brand-kit/about-brand-kit/) is a cutting-edge platform designed to ensure AI-generated content aligns with a customer's brand, style, and messaging. It enables the creation of brand-specific profiles and provides access to relevant company information, ensuring consistent, on-brand AI-generated content.
