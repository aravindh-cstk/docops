# What are Voice Profiles?

[Voice Profiles](/docs/brand-kit/about-voice-profile/) in Contentstack's Brand Kit enables you to create custom guidelines for the tone, style, and language of the AI-generated content. This ensures that your brand's voice remains authentic and engaging across all digital touchpoints.
