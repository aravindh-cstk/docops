# What kind of content should I avoid including in my Knowledge Vault?

You should avoid including irrelevant, outdated, confusing, sensitive, or mislabeled data that could introduce errors or inconsistencies in AI-generated content. You should try including only accurate, up-to-date, and clearly labeled brand assets and information to maintain the integrity and coherence of your digital presence.
