# How are Brand Kits associated with Stacks?

In Contentstack, a Brand Kit can be associated with one or more stacks, allowing consistent branding across multiple digital properties. Each Brand Kit is connected to a single Knowledge Vault, providing a centralized source of truth for all brand assets and guidelines.
