# What are the possible use cases for Personalize?

Here are some possible use cases for Contentstack Personalize:

-   **Visitor Authentication:** Showing different cards based on whether the visitor is an authenticated user or not.
-   **Product Promotion**: Displaying promotional material for a product an existing customer has not purchased before.
-   **Geolocation**: Showing different shipping options via cards depending on which is offered by the visitor's current selected store.
-   **A/B Testing**: Testing different layouts, text, graphics, etc. for a campaign landing page to measure lead generation effectiveness.
-   **Device Recognition**: Changing graphics displayed based on the device used by the visitor (mobile vs. web).
-   **UTM Parameters**: Personalizing content for marketing campaigns to identify specific channels and content that drive the most engagement.
