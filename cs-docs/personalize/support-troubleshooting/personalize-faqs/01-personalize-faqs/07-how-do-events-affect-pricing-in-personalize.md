# How do events affect pricing in Personalize?

Pricing for **Personalize** is based on **Data & Insights (Lytics) events**, not on the number of personalization experiences or variants delivered.

**What counts as an event:**

-   Each page view or tracked user action (such as clicks, form submissions, or video plays) generates an event.
-   These events are stored in **Lytics** and form the basis for usage measurement.

**How pricing is calculated:**

-   Your cost depends on the number of **Data & Insights (Lytics) events** collected, regardless of how many personalization rules or variants you use.
-   The number of variants delivered **does not** impact pricing.
